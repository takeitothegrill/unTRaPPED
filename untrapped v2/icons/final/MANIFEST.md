## How to tell them apart in Google's icon picker

The Photos picker shows **no filenames** — you match by eye. Identify by shape
and glyph, then by dot/body colour:

| Family | Shape | Glyph | Where the rating colour is |
|---|---|---|---|
| parking | blue teardrop pin | white **P** | small dot, bottom-right |
| toilet | blue teardrop pin | white **T** | small dot, bottom-right |
| route | circle | white dynamic wheelchair | the whole circle |
| venue | rounded square | white seated wheelchair | the whole square |

Note that parking and toilet differ only by one letter at 128px — check the
glyph, not the colour.
